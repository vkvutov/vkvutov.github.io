About

My work sits where machine learning meets engineering. I care about choosing models for good reasons: interpretable GAMs over black boxes when inspection matters, proper hypothesis tests for drift, and held-out test sets that stay untouched until the very end. I also insist on wrapping models in config-driven, tracked, containerized pipelines.

My PhD research focused on developing novel analytical methods for matrix-assisted laser desorption/ionization mass spectrometry imaging (MALDI-MSI) data. I'm particularly interested in interpretable models, uncertainty quantification, boosting methods, and causal inference.

Lately I've been working on LLM retrieval systems (hybrid dense + lexical search, contextual embeddings, LLM evaluation), systematic hyperparameter tuning for vision transformers, and NLP applications.

A few reads and ideas that have really shaped how I think about problems: Leo Breiman's classic "Two Cultures" paper, Jerome Friedman's work on gradient boosting, Trevor Hastie and Robert Tibshirani's foundational work on GAMs, and Simon Wood's work on smoothing and GAMs.

In my spare time: 🎣 fishing · 🥾 hiking · ⚽ football · 🎶 vinyl · 📚 novels

Projects

Paper Search

paper-search

Hybrid semantic search and Q&A over research papers. Combines FAISS dense retrieval with BM25 via reciprocal rank fusion, enriches chunks with LLM-generated context, answers with a local Qwen model, and scores answers with a validator pass. Includes a Streamlit UI and a systematic benchmark suite.

ViT for Breast Histopathology

ViT-histo

Fine-tuning a Vision Transformer for tumor / no-tumor classification of tissue patches, following Google's Deep Learning Tuning Playbook: quasi-random sweeps, scientific vs. nuisance parameters, checkpoint selection and an untouched test set.

Test ROC-AUC 0.96 · Brier 0.070
